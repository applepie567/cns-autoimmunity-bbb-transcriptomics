# Analysis strengthening protocol, 7 September 2026

The earlier v2.0.0 freeze is preserved. These analyses were added after inspection of the existing findings and are not a preregistered study.

## Matched reference
Use the archived 7,705 strict reciprocal orthologues in every mouse pair and mouse to human comparison. Estimate Hedges g in each cohort and use the original random effects model for pooled mouse effects. Resample biological subjects within each disease and control stratum with replacement for 2,000 iterations. Recalculate all comparisons in the same draw so dependence between comparisons is retained. Exclude undefined effects jointly from all comparisons in that draw and report coverage. Also omit each animal or donor in turn. Do not use gene count based P values as evidence of biological replication. Summarize the mean of the three mouse pair correlations and the mean of the three individual mouse to human correlations, and their difference. This conditional comparison cannot isolate species, anatomical site, disease stage or platform effects.

## Human validation
Use Macnair et al., Neuron 2025, cohort I in Zenodo 8338963. Retain published Endocyte_Arterial, Endocyte_Capillary, Endocyte_Venous and Endocyte_Stressed annotations. Exclude source labelled mixed clusters and all nuclei marked exclude_pseudobulk. Restrict to white matter. Primary comparison is chronic active lesions (CAL) versus healthy white matter (WM), with at least 20 endothelial nuclei per donor and lesion class. This threshold was selected during metadata screening before calculating validation effects. Use 10 nuclei as a sensitivity threshold. Sum counts within donor and lesion class, then normalize to log counts per million. Estimate gene centering and scaling from the primary all endothelial comparison and retain those same weights in every subtype and donor sensitivity analysis. Assess the three original programs. Apply exact donor label permutations and BH correction within the three program tests. Compare endothelial subtype proportions separately with BH correction across four subtypes.

Repeat with source annotated capillary endothelial nuclei using the same minimum cell thresholds. Also repeat the all endothelial analysis in exactly the donors eligible for the capillary analysis, to separate the change in eligible donors from the restriction in cell type. Other vascular subtypes lack adequate healthy donor replication at 20 nuclei and will not support separate disease inference. Report both the general gene overlap and the common genes between the all EC and capillary comparisons. Gene effects require CPM at least 1 in at least 3 eligible validation donors and finite standardized effects. For final concordance intervals, jointly resample animals from all three mouse cohorts, donors from GSE279183 and eligible donors from the validation cohort. Recompute both sides of each comparison. Count degenerate draws explicitly. Program score ranges resample validation donors with fixed gene standardization.

Repeat the all EC comparison using Amsterdam brain bank donors only to check tissue bank sensitivity. The primary eligible CAL and healthy donors are from Amsterdam and Edinburgh. The original GSE279183 tissue source is the UK Multiple Sclerosis Tissue Bank. Capillary restriction also changes eligible donors, so any attenuation is not proof that composition alone caused the original signal.

GSE180759 was also screened. Its deposited vascular label mixes endothelial and mural cells. Apply the original endothelial marker rule without a permissive fallback. Healthy control endothelial counts are 0, 8 and 0. The dataset does not provide a suitable healthy endothelial comparison and will not be presented as successful independent disease validation.

## Bootstrap estimability
The fixed gene benchmark produced 1,872 estimable draws from 2,000 attempted resamples. In 128 draws, resampling the very small strata removed the variance needed for standardized effects. These draws were not replaced and are counted in the audit. Percentile ranges condition on the estimable draws and are descriptive rather than evidence of a pure species effect. Valid draws retain 7,055 to 7,700 genes, with a median of 7,608. All comparisons within a draw use exactly the same genes. Leave one subject out estimates provide a separate sensitivity analysis.
